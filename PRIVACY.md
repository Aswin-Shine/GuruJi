# GuruJi Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 23 September 2026

GuruJi is an AI study helper for students in Class 5 to 10. It answers
questions using NCERT textbook content, on WhatsApp and on the web. GuruJi is
in a **closed pilot**: only invited students can use it.

This policy explains what information GuruJi collects, why, who else handles
it, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted. Most GuruJi users are
children, so it is written to be read by a parent or guardian.

Contact for anything in this policy: **ashwin.sh91@gmail.com**

---

## 1. What we collect

| Information | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| **Phone number** (WhatsApp number or the number used to log in on the web) | To identify the student's account. |
| **Class (5–10)**, board (NCERT) and language preference | So answers match the right textbook and level. |
| **Display name and avatar**, if the student sets them on the web | Shown in the student's own profile. Optional. |
| **Questions the student asks and GuruJi's answers** | To show the student's chat history, keep a conversation going, and let a linked parent review flagged exchanges. |
| **A short learning profile**: topics understood, topics the student finds hard, and preferred explanation style | So later answers can build on what the student already knows. It is regenerated automatically from recent conversations. |
| **Safety flags**: the text of any message that our safety check blocked | So a linked parent can see that something was blocked and talk to their child about it. |
| **Parent link**, if a student links a parent's phone number | So the parent can see a progress summary and any flagged exchanges. |
| **Technical records**: WhatsApp message IDs (to avoid answering the same message twice) and usage-cost totals | To run the service reliably. Cost records contain no personal data. |

**Photos of questions** (web only, when enabled) are checked for safety and
turned into text. **The photo itself is never stored.** It is discarded as soon
as the text is read, and only the text of the question is kept, like a typed
question.

**We do not collect** location, contacts, email addresses, payment details,
or anything from other apps. GuruJi has **no advertising, no analytics or
tracking tools, and no cookies**. The web app keeps its login in the browser
tab's session storage, which is cleared when the tab is closed.

## 2. How we use it

Only to run GuruJi: to answer questions, keep the student's history and
learning profile, keep the service safe for children, and let a linked parent
see how their child is doing.

We **do not sell** personal information, **do not use it for advertising**,
and **do not use it to train AI models**.

## 3. Who else handles it

GuruJi relies on three service providers. Each receives only what it needs
for its part of the job.

| Provider | What they receive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **OpenAI** (USA) | The text of each question, recent conversation context, the learning profile, and photos of questions when that feature is used. **Not** the phone number. | To check messages for safety, understand the question, and write the answer. |
| **Meta / WhatsApp** | Messages sent to and from GuruJi on WhatsApp, and the phone number. | WhatsApp delivers the messages. Meta's own privacy policy applies to your use of WhatsApp. |
| **Amazon Web Services** (Mumbai, India region) | All stored data, on our server and database. | Hosting. |

Under OpenAI's API data-usage terms at the time of writing, data sent through
its API is not used to train its models by default, and OpenAI may keep it for
a limited period for abuse monitoring.

We do not share personal information with anyone else, except where the law
requires it.

## 4. Who can see a student's information

- **The student** can see their own conversations.
- **A linked parent** can see a progress summary and any **flagged
  exchanges**. A parent **cannot** read the student's full chat history.
- **The GuruJi operator** can access the database and server logs to run and
  fix the service, and to handle requests under this policy.

When a student deletes a conversation in the app, it is **hidden from the
student but not erased**, so that flagged exchanges stay available for parent
review. Permanent erasure is described in section 6.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Account, conversations, learning profile, safety flags and parent links**
  are kept for as long as the account exists, until deletion is requested
  (section 6). During the pilot there is no automatic expiry.
- **Server logs**, which can include the phone number and the text of
  GuruJi's replies, are kept in small rotating files and are overwritten
  automatically, usually within days to weeks depending on usage.
- **Photos** are never stored (section 1).

## 6. Your rights: access, correction and deletion

A student, or their parent or guardian, can ask us to:

- **see** the information we hold about the student,
- **correct** it (the class and profile details can also be changed in the
  app), or
- **delete** it permanently.

Email **ashwin.sh91@gmail.com** from, or naming, the phone number on the
account. A deletion permanently erases the account, all conversations, the
learning profile, safety flags and parent links, in one step. Records already
sent to a provider in section 3 are then governed by that provider's own
retention terms. We will confirm when the deletion is done.

Consent to use GuruJi can be withdrawn at any time. Stop using it and ask for
deletion as above.

## 7. Children

GuruJi is built for children aged roughly 10 to 16. During the pilot, students
join **by invitation only**, and a parent or guardian should agree to their
child using GuruJi before they start. If you are a parent and believe your
child is using GuruJi without your agreement, contact us and we will delete
the account.

Every message is checked by an automated safety filter before it reaches the
AI and again before an answer is sent. Blocked messages are recorded (section 1)
so a linked parent can review them.

## 8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on a private server.
Access to the pilot website is password-protected, and messages from WhatsApp
are accepted only when they are cryptographically signed by Meta. No system is
perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and
the relevant authority as the law requires.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a
new "Last updated" date. For significant changes affecting how a child's data
is used, we will tell invited families directly.

## 10. Contact and grievances

Questions, requests or complaints about this policy or how GuruJi handles
personal data: **ashwin.sh91@gmail.com**. We aim to respond within 7 days.

This policy is intended to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act,
2023. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
